Brainrot Mayhem Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

This policy explains what data the Android game Brainrot Mayhem (package br.com.whalecoderz.brainrotmayhem) handles, why, who it is shared with and how you can exercise your rights. It follows Brazil's General Data Protection Law (LGPD, Law 13,709/2018), the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play's policies. If this English version and the Portuguese version differ, the Portuguese version prevails.

Summary. The game has no sign-up, does not ask for your name, email or phone number and uses no device permissions (location, camera, microphone, contacts or files). Your progress is saved on your device. To fix bugs, understand how the game is played and show rewarded ads that you choose to watch, the game uses Google services, which receive technical device data and identifiers. The rewarded ads come through Appodeal, an ad mediation service that calls several ad networks, which also receive technical device data and identifiers (section 3.7). To learn which ad each install came from, the Google Play version also uses AppsFlyer. We do not sell data.

1. Who is responsible

The data controller is Gustavo G. Fucilini, the developer who publishes the game as WhaleCoderz, based in Brazil. For any privacy matter, including the requests in section 8, write to whalecoderz@gmail.com. This address is the contact channel for data subjects and serves as the data protection officer contact (LGPD art. 41).

2. Data that stays on your device

The game profile is saved in a file in the app's internal storage and is not sent to us. It contains a nickname generated by the game (for example, "Cozinheiro 1234"), coins and Cannoli (in-game currencies with no cash value), characters, upgrades, achievements, missions, records and settings (music and sound volume, controls, screen shake, damage numbers). Uninstalling the game deletes this file.

If Android Backup is turned on on your device, Android may copy this file to your Google Account under Google's terms. You control this in Settings > Google > Backup.

3. Data handled by third-party services

The services in 3.1 to 3.5 are provided by Google, the one in 3.6 by AppsFlyer and the one in 3.7 by Appodeal, which brings in other ad networks. They receive data directly from the device. Google's services also handle it under the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

3.1 Google Firebase Crashlytics (crash reports)

When the game crashes, it sends a report with: the error stack trace and message, device model and manufacturer, Android version, game version, memory and disk space, screen orientation, whether the device is rooted, a Crashlytics installation identifier, the Firebase installation identifier and the game context at that moment (open screen, map and minute of the run). We use this only to find and fix bugs.

3.2 Google Analytics for Firebase (usage statistics)

The game logs gameplay events: run start and end, map, character and modes chosen, result, duration, level, kills, coins, Cannoli earned and spent, upgrades bought with in-game coins, build items, fusions, second chances, achievements, Macchinetta use, rewarded ads watched and performance (frames per second and hitches). Analytics also collects on its own: an app instance identifier, device model, Android and game version, language, approximate country and region (derived from the IP address, which Analytics masks), first open date, sessions, screen views and engagement time. We use this to balance difficulty, improve performance and learn whether players enjoy the game. We do not use this data to identify you.

Analytics also collects the Android advertising ID and is linked to our Google Ads account, to measure the campaigns that promote the game and to build audiences from the events above (for example, so the game's ad is not shown to people who already installed it). In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, this follows your consent for ads (section 3.3).

3.3 Google AdMob and Google User Messaging Platform (ads)

The game only shows rewarded ads, and only when you tap a button to watch one (for example, to get a second chance). There are no banners and no ads that appear on their own. AdMob collects: the Android advertising ID, the app set ID and, where applicable, identifiers related to accounts signed in on the device; the IP address, which gives a rough idea of the region; interactions (game launch, taps, video views) and performance data (launch time, hangs, energy use). Google uses this data to serve and measure ads, for statistics and to prevent fraud, and may use it for personalized ads where the law allows. AdMob is one of the ad networks that Appodeal calls (section 3.7).

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent first through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). Appodeal's consent manager uses the same answer (section 3.7). You can change your choice at any time in SETTINGS > Privacy inside the game.
  • In any country, you can delete or reset your advertising ID in Android Settings > Privacy > Ads. Without it, ads are no longer personalized.

3.4 Google Play Games Services (cloud save)

If you sign in to Play Games, the game stores a copy of your profile (section 2) in Google Play Saved Games, so you can restore your progress on a new phone or play on more than one device. The game may receive your Play Games player ID and player name from Google: they stay on the device, and the game does not send them to us or to third parties. The game does not receive your email address. Play Games itself collects usage and diagnostics data to improve the service, under Google's policy. You can play without signing in, and you can delete the game's saved data in the Play Games app settings or in your Google Account.

3.5 Purchases

The game currently sells nothing. If in-app purchases are added, they will be processed by Google Play: we never receive card or payment details, and this policy will be updated first.

3.6 AppsFlyer (which ad an install came from)

The version of the game downloaded from Google Play uses AppsFlyer, by AppsFlyer Ltd. (Israel), to learn whether an install came from one of our ads and which one, and to measure the campaigns on the ad networks where we promote the game. AppsFlyer receives: an AppsFlyer install identifier, the Android advertising ID, the install referrer (the link or ad the install came from, as Google Play reports it and, when present, Samsung or the Facebook and Instagram apps), the IP address (which gives a rough idea of the region), device model, Android and game version, language, game opens and an event when you reach levels 5, 10, 15 and so on in a run. To prevent ad fraud it also receives technical data such as launch time and battery level. The game does not send AppsFlyer your nickname, email, phone number or any account identifier.

AppsFlyer handles this data as a processor, on our behalf, under its services privacy policy, and does not sell it. It sends the ad network an install came from the result of the measurement (for example, that its ad brought an install or a level reached). It only starts after Google's messaging platform answers: in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, only with your consent, which you can withdraw in SETTINGS > Privacy.

After the same consent, AppsFlyer also receives, for each ad you watch, the ad network, the amount earned (in US dollars), the ad unit and type and the place in the game where it was shown. The game takes these from Appodeal's ad revenue report.

3.7 Appodeal and its ad networks (ad mediation)

The rewarded ads are requested through Appodeal, the ad mediation service of Appodeal, Inc. (a Delaware corporation, United States): it asks several ad networks for an ad and shows the one chosen. Appodeal handles data under its own privacy policy, and publishes a list of its partners' privacy policies.

What Appodeal collects. According to Appodeal's own disclosure for its Android SDK: ad impressions and clicks; diagnostic data; technical signals (device model, memory, storage and user agent); and the advertising ID and similar identifiers, the IP address (which gives a rough idea of the region), the mobile country and network code, the carrier and the connection type. Appodeal says it collects location only if the app has the location permission, a user ID only if the app sets one and purchases only if the app reports them. The game has no location permission, sets no user ID in Appodeal, reports no purchases and has no email or phone number to give it.

The ad networks. Appodeal calls the networks listed below (besides Google AdMob, in section 3.3) and runs three other mediations inside it (AppLovin MAX, Bidon and LevelPlay), which call networks of their own. Each link goes to the page where that network describes the data it collects:

BidMachine, Unity Ads, Liftoff Monetize (Vungle), Moloco, AppLovin (MAX), Meta Audience Network, Mintegral, Pangle, Bigo Ads, Chartboost, Amazon Publisher Services, DT Exchange (Fyber), InMobi, LevelPlay (ironSource), MobileFuse, myTarget, Ogury, PubMatic, Smaato, Start.io, TaurusX, Verve, Yandex Mobile Ads, zMaticoo, Bidon.

Depending on the network, these pages say they collect: device information (such as model, system, screen, memory, language and time zone), the advertising ID and the app set ID, the IP address (which gives a rough idea of the region), network and carrier, ad impressions and clicks, and crash and performance data. Some networks' policies also list other device identifiers (for example, the Android ID) and location only with the app's permission, which the game does not request. They use this data to serve and measure ads, for analytics and to prevent fraud, and they may share it with their own partners, such as advertisers and measurement companies, as their policies describe. Two of the networks start their own analytics, which send data to the company that owns them: AppMetrica, by Yandex (details), and MyTracker, by VK (details).

Consent. Appodeal only starts after Google's messaging platform (UMP) has answered and, in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, after you have made your choice there. Appodeal's consent manager (Stack Consent Manager) is built on the same UMP: it reads the answer you gave and, if it still needs to show its own consent form, the game shows that form before the ads start. You can review your choice at any time in SETTINGS > Privacy inside the game, where the line is shown in the countries to which Google's message applies. In any country, you can reset the advertising ID in Android (section 3.3).

5. Children and teenagers

Brainrot Mayhem is intended for people aged 13 and over and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child used the game and had data collected, write to whalecoderz@gmail.com: we will delete what is within our reach and ask the services in section 3 to delete it.

For teenagers, we handle data in their best interest (LGPD art. 14): the game has no chat, shows no player's data to other players, asks for no contact details and sends no notifications. We recommend that players under 18 use the game with guidance from a parent or guardian.

6. International transfers

Google services may process data on servers outside Brazil, including in the United States. These transfers rely on the safeguards Google adopts, such as standard contractual clauses (LGPD art. 33 and GDPR art. 46). AppsFlyer may also process data outside Brazil, including in the United States, under standard contractual clauses. Appodeal and the ad networks in 3.7 may also process data outside Brazil, including in the United States: their own policies describe where and under which safeguards.

7. How long we keep data

  • On-device profile: until you uninstall the game or clear the app's data.
  • Crashlytics crash reports: 90 days, then deletion starts.
  • Analytics data tied to the device identifier: 14 months, then deleted automatically. Aggregated reports, which identify no one, remain.
  • AdMob data: for the periods Google sets in its own policy.
  • AppsFlyer data tied to the device: 24 months, then deleted.
  • Appodeal and ad network data: for the periods each sets in its own policy (some say up to 2 years, such as AppLovin, Mintegral and Bigo Ads, and MobileFuse says 90 days).
  • Play Games saved games: until you delete them or delete your Google Account.

8. Your rights

You have the right to confirm whether we process your data, access it, correct it, ask for anonymization, blocking or deletion, portability, information about sharing, withdraw consent and object to processing (LGPD art. 18; GDPR arts. 15 to 21). To do so:

  • Delete the on-device profile: uninstall the game or clear the app's data in Android.
  • Ads, Appodeal and AppsFlyer: in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, SETTINGS > Privacy in the game; in any country, the advertising ID in Android. To delete AppsFlyer or Appodeal data, write to us: we pass the request on to them. Many of the ad networks' pages in 3.7 also say how to ask them directly.
  • Play Games: delete the game's data in the Play Games app or your Google Account.
  • Any other request: write to whalecoderz@gmail.com. We reply within 15 days. Since there are no accounts, we may ask for details that help us find the data (for example, the device model and roughly when you played).

You can also complain to Brazil's National Data Protection Authority (ANPD) or to the data protection authority in your country.

9. Security

Data sent to Google services and to AppsFlyer travels encrypted (HTTPS). Not every ad network says it encrypts data in transit, so this does not cover Appodeal and its networks. The profile is stored in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. No system is completely secure, but we limit what we collect to what the purposes above need.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will publish the new version on this page with its update date. If the change is significant (for example, a new type of data or a new service), we will also announce it inside the game.
